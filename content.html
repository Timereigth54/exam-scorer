3. Attach a recording
Wait until the current external generation job has finished. Produce images first, then audio. Use the main cleared cast and only permitted additional voices. Upload one assembled MP3 with the required instructions, pauses, and recording repetitions already included.





After upload, wait at least 90 seconds before verifying so storage can propagate. Verification must succeed before the recording is offered to students.
